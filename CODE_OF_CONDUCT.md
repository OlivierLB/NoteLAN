# Code of conduct

NoteLAN aims to be a welcoming and constructive project.

When participating, please:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- focus feedback on the work, not the person;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private information;
- keep technical discussions relevant and actionable;
- respect the privacy of vault content and local network information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/OlivierLB).
